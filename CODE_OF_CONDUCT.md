# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct / 贡献者公约行为准则

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

我们作为社区成员、贡献者和负责人，承诺为所有人提供不受骚扰的参与体验，不因年龄、体型、显性或隐性残障、族裔、性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、阶层、肤色、宗教、性身份或性取向而区别对待。

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

我们承诺以有助于建立开放、友好、多元、包容和健康社区的方式行动与交流。

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community

有助于营造积极环境的行为包括：

- 对他人表现出同理心和善意
- 尊重不同的意见、观点和经历
- 提供并坦然接受建设性反馈
- 为自己的错误负责，并向受影响的人道歉
- 以整个社区的最大利益为出发点

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

不可接受的行为包括：

- 使用色情化语言或图像，以及任何形式的性关注或性骚扰
- 挑衅、侮辱、贬损性评论，以及人身或政治攻击
- 公开或私下骚扰
- 未经明确许可公开他人的私人信息
- 其他在专业环境中可被合理认定为不当的行为

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

社区负责人负责解释和执行这些标准，并会对不当、威胁性、冒犯性或有害行为采取适当且公平的纠正措施。

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

社区负责人有权并有责任删除、编辑或拒绝不符合本准则的评论、提交、代码、Wiki 编辑、Issue 及其他贡献，并会在适当时说明管理决定的理由。

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces and when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

本准则适用于所有社区空间，也适用于个人在公共场合正式代表本社区时的行为。

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the maintainers via GitHub. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Community leaders must respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

如遇辱骂、骚扰或其他不可接受行为，请通过 GitHub 私下向维护者报告。所有投诉都会得到及时、公正的审查与调查。社区负责人必须保护报告人的隐私和安全。

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining consequences:

1. **Correction**: A private written warning for inappropriate language or behavior, with an explanation of the violation.
2. **Warning**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior, including no interaction with the people involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary Ban**: A temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent Ban**: A permanent ban from public interaction within the community for sustained violations or harassment.

社区负责人将依据以下社区影响指引决定处理方式：

1. **纠正**：针对不当语言或行为发出私下书面提醒，并说明违反的规则。
2. **警告**：对持续行为发出带有后果的警告，并可在指定期限内禁止与相关人员互动。
3. **临时封禁**：临时禁止与社区进行任何互动或公开交流。
4. **永久封禁**：对于持续违规或骚扰行为，永久禁止在社区内公开互动。
